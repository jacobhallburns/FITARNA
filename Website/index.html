Augmented Reality Navigation for Evans Library.

FITARNA helps students and visitors navigate Evans Library using augmented reality directions, interactive pop-ups, and self-guided tour stops.

Core Features

A better way to explore the library.

FITARNA combines indoor navigation, AR overlays, and contextual information so users can find destinations and learn about important library spaces as they move.

01

AR Navigation

Users choose a destination from a dropdown or search option, then follow AR directions through the library.

02

Tour Mode

A self-guided tour leads users through important stops in Evans Library, including study areas, services, and resources.

03

Interactive Pop-ups

Pop-ups appear near points of interest and can provide information, links, quiz questions, and tour progression.

Inside the App

Actual Tour Mode interface.

FITARNA displays AR pop-ups over the live camera view, allowing users to move through guided tour stops and learn about library spaces as they walk.

  • Live AR View: Camera feed stays visible while content appears in the environment.
  • Guided Tour Route: Lead users through the tour one location at a time.
  • Relevant Location Details: Display useful information when users approach important library spaces.
